Introduction

The Philippines is home to some of the most stunning natural wonders in Southeast Asia. From white sand beaches to ancient rice terraces, here are five spots every traveler should see.

El Nido

El Nido is a municipality in Palawan known for its breathtaking natural beauty. The area is home to numerous limestone cliffs, pristine beaches, and crystal-clear waters, making it a popular destination for snorkeling, diving, and island hopping.

People's Park in the Sky

People's Park in the Sky is a popular tourist destination in Tagaytay City known for its stunning panoramic views of the surrounding mountains and valleys. The park offers a peaceful retreat for visitors seeking to enjoy nature and take in the beauty of the region.

Baguio City

Baguio City is a mountainous city in the Philippines known for its colonial architecture and cool climate. It is a popular destination for tourists seeking a respite from the heat and humidity of the lowlands.

Museo de Baler

Museo de Baler is a museum located in Baler, Aurora province, known for its distinctive architecture and collection of historical artifacts. The museum serves as a repository of local culture and history, offering visitors a glimpse into the rich heritage of the region.

Banaue Rice Terraces

2,000-year-old engineering marvels carved by hand into the mountains of Ifugao, Philippines by the ancestors of the indigenous Igorot people. Often referred to as the "Eighth Wonder of the World," these stone and mud-walled terraces sit roughly 1,500 meters above sea level and would stretch halfway around the globe if placed end-to-end. While the terraces directly surrounding the Banaue town center are the most accessible, the Ifugao region boasts several distinct terrace clusters that offer completely different viewpoints and experiences.
